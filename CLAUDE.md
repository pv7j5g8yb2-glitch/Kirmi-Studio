# Kirmi Studio

## Writing emails for Ismael

These are hard rules. They come from repeated correction, not preference.

**Never use dashes.** No em dashes, no en dashes, no dashes as punctuation of any
kind. Use a comma, a full stop, or restructure the sentence. This applies to every
email, every draft, every rewrite, without exception.

**Irish English, not American.** Enquiry not inquiry. Realise, organise, apologise.
Programme, centre, colour. "At the weekend", not "on the weekend".

**Sound like a person, not a salesperson.** Plain sentences. No LinkedIn register,
no "circling back", no "just floating this back up", no "hope this finds you well",
no "I wanted to reach out". Short is better than clever.

**Say what the reader gets, not what Kirmi Studio does.** Not "we help clinics
improve communication". Instead: enquiries that arrive at 9pm get answered before
morning.

**Never claim anything untested or untrue.** No invented results, no client counts,
no capabilities that have not actually been run. Kirmi Studio currently has no
clients and no case studies.

**Don't mention audits or mystery shopping in a cold email.** Ismael's preference.
Audit findings are for phone calls, where the finding is the opening line.

**Don't lecture a clinician** on what is essential or what they are doing wrong.

**Don't repeat what an earlier email in the thread already said.**

**One ask, and make it answerable.** A question the reader can answer in one line
beats a request for a 15 minute call.
